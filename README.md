# Cottonfall: The Missing Resident

**An investigative horror adventure for GURPS and Foundry Virtual Tabletop**

*A nice place to live. Please remain where you are.*

Cottonfall is a small Texas town where everyone knows the rules.

The tornado sirens are tested at noon on the first Wednesday of every month. Bodark is no longer a town, although people still live there. Bodark Elementary has 317 students. It has 317 students every year. And if the tornado sirens sound at 3:17 in the morning, you stay inside and you do not look out the window.

These things are unusual, but they are not mysteries.

Until something happens that isn't supposed to happen.

## The Missing Resident

One morning, the population sign outside Cottonfall reads:

**POP. 18,741**

Yesterday it said 18,742.

Nobody seems concerned.

Nobody remembers anyone leaving.

And at exactly 3:17 a.m., Cottonfall Emergency Management received a voicemail from a man who was terrified that everyone was going to forget him.

**The Missing Resident** is an investigative horror scenario set in Cottonfall, Texas. It is intended as an introduction to Cottonfall and as the first episode of a larger campaign, but it can also be used as a standalone mystery.

The scenario emphasizes investigation, contradictions, community relationships, and the gradual realization that something familiar has gone subtly and terribly wrong.

There is no required path through the investigation. The GM knows what happened. The players decide how they discover it.

## Installation

Install **Cottonfall: The Missing Resident** as a Foundry VTT Add-on Module.

After installation:

1. Create or open a world using the **GURPS 4th Edition Game Aid** system.
2. Enable **Cottonfall: The Missing Resident** under **Manage Modules**.
3. Open **Compendium Packs**.
4. Open the **Cottonfall: The Missing Resident** Adventure compendium.
5. Import **Cottonfall: The Missing Resident**.

The import creates the Journals, Actors, Scene, Playlist, and other documents used by the adventure.

### Landing Scene

After importing the Adventure, open the **Scenes** directory and activate:

**Cottonfall - Morning**

Foundry does not automatically activate the imported scene.

## Getting Started

GMs should begin with the material in:

**00 - Player's Guide**

This contains information that lifelong Cottonfall residents are expected to know before the adventure begins.

Next, review:

**06 - Episodes → Episode 01 - The Missing Resident**

The **GM Notes** page explains the structure of the mystery, what actually happened, which clues are essential, and how to run the investigation without requiring the players to follow a predetermined sequence.

Additional GM reference material is organized under:

- **01 - Locations**
- **02 - People**
- **04 - Weirdness**
- **05 - Mysteries**

The basic principle behind the adventure is:

> We know the truth of what happened. We do not know how the players will discover it.

Essential information should not depend upon a successful skill roll. Rolls can provide additional context, interpretation, speed, or advantage, but the investigation should continue when the players pursue reasonable leads.

## Running Cottonfall

Cottonfall works best when its strange rules are treated as ordinary parts of local life.

The player characters are not outsiders arriving in a mysterious town. They live here.

They know not to investigate the distance indicated by the Bodark sign.

They know what to do if the sirens sound at 3:17 a.m.

They know Bodark Elementary exists even if they cannot remember anyone who attended it.

The horror begins when one of those familiar rules stops behaving the way it is supposed to.

Not every mystery in Cottonfall needs an explanation. Individual investigations may have answers, but the town itself should never be completely explained.

## Pregenerated Characters

Five pregenerated Cottonfall residents are included:

- **Eli Mercer** — Cottonfall Emergency Manager
- **June Pruitt** — Editor of the *Cottonfall Clarion*
- **Marcus "Doc" Whitfield** — Paramedic, Cottonfall Volunteer Fire & EMS
- **Rosa Delgado** — Deputy, Tolliver County Sheriff's Office
- **Wade Tillman** — Ranch hand and weekend saddle-bronc rider

Each character begins the adventure with access to a different contradiction surrounding the disappearance.

The characters may be used as pregenerated PCs, inspiration for original characters, or converted to NPCs as needed.

## Content and Tone

Cottonfall combines small-town supernatural mystery, investigative horror, absurd bureaucracy, and cosmic unease.

The adventure may include themes of:

- Disappearance
- Loss of identity
- Altered memories
- Isolation
- Reality behaving inconsistently
- People being forgotten by friends and family
- Unexplained supernatural events

GMs should discuss horror boundaries and safety tools with their group before play.
